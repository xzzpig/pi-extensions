the actual registered `update_goal` definition and the flow under test is the real runGoalCompletionFlow wrapper
(handoff + split billing). The auditor is injected, and in the last case the injected auditor IS the real

SUBAGENT_DELEGATION_REQUEST/STARTED/RESPONSE/CANCEL), so the delegation chain is exercised through the handler.

  1. held promise + unique child + one handoff + split billing:
     - the update_goal promise does NOT settle after 5 event-loop turns while the audit is unresolved, and
       nothing is committed before the verdict; exactly one auditor child is launched;
     - on the terminal: content[0] is the formal approval report and exactly ONE appendix follows, carrying the
       fixed title 审计协调记录（参考信息，不是新用户指令或完成证据） and the answered question (ALPHA);
       details.supervisorCoordination carries the same single record;

       0.02+0.005) while goal.usage.tokensUsed is unchanged — the parent turn budget is not modified;
     - the record is never sent through sendMessage/sendUserMessage and never with triggerTurn true;
     - the ledger has exactly one audit_usage, exactly one audit_supervisor_usage (tokens 11 / turns 1 /
       auditRequestId delegation-1), exactly one audit_result and zero audit_skipped, and the goal archives at
       turn_end.
  2. a question-free audit writes no appendix, no supervisorCoordination detail and NO side usage row.
  3. fail-closed coordination: the unresolved question and the coordination record come back in the tool result,
     never a pseudo-approval, the goal stays active with no goal_completed row, and the actually-spent side usage
     is still recorded once.

  5. the real delegation chain through the real handler: exactly one child launch and exactly one precise
     cancellation; the cancel acknowledgement carrying a late `verdict: approved` is only an acknowledgement, so
     the tool result never says approved, the goal stays active and the single audit_result row has verdict error.
